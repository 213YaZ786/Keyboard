# Keyboard

Keyboard for Android, a fork of [HeliBoard](https://github.com/HeliBorg/HeliBoard).

## Credits

- [HeliBoard](https://github.com/HeliBorg/HeliBoard) (GPL-3.0), itself based on
  [OpenBoard](https://github.com/openboard-team/openboard) and the
  [AOSP keyboard](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/packages/inputmethods/LatinIME/)
  (Apache-2.0). Their copyright notices stay in each file.
- [KeyboardKit](https://github.com/KeyboardKit/KeyboardKit) 9.9.1 (MIT): the
  callouts and the suggestion bar.
- [FlorisBoard](https://github.com/florisboard/florisboard) (Apache-2.0): the
  clipboard layout and the glide typing classifier.
- [Scribe-Android](https://github.com/scribe-org/Scribe-Android) (GPL-3.0): the
  CMake build of the dictionary engine.
- [URLCheck](https://github.com/TrianguloY/URLCheck) (CC BY 4.0): the
  cleaning of pasted links, with the tracking rules of
  [ClearURLs](https://github.com/ClearURLs/Rules) (LGPL-3.0) and the
  [Public Suffix List](https://publicsuffix.org) (MPL-2.0).
- Dictionaries from [aosp-dictionaries](https://codeberg.org/Helium314/aosp-dictionaries):
  Arabic from [aosp-dictionary-tools](https://github.com/remi0s/aosp-dictionary-tools)
  (Apache-2.0); Kabyle from word lists of [OPUS](https://opus.nlpl.eu/)
  (CC BY 4.0).
- Emoji search dictionaries (English, French, Arabic, Kabyle, German,
  Spanish) from [aosp-dictionaries](https://codeberg.org/Helium314/aosp-dictionaries):
  words from [CLDR](https://cldr.unicode.org) (Unicode License v3) and the
  [emoji-search-index of Signal](https://github.com/signalapp/emoji-search-index)
  (AGPL-3.0); these data files stay under the AGPL-3.0.
- [Noto Animated Emoji](https://googlefonts.github.io/noto-emoji-animation/)
  (Google, CC BY 4.0): the animated emoji, resized to 160 px.
